Questions

 

What do I need?

Your architectural plans as PDFs, a Mac or PC, and a Claude or Codex subscription. The assistant does the reading, building and research. Viewing a finished tour needs only a web browser.

How long does the first build take?

Longer than you might expect: often a few hours of assistant time for a full drawing set. It reads every page, transcribes each dimension, checks that they add up, then red-teams the result. After that, changes and new layers are quick.

Where do my documents go?

Nowhere. Plans, photos, insurance papers and surveys stay in the folder on your computer. This site never sees them, and the tour works offline.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as your drawings. Every dimension must trace to a printed number, and anything that doesn't is labeled as assumed. It is a visualization and planning tool, not a construction document; your architect's drawings still govern.

Can I change the layout or add on?

Yes. Describe the change (an addition, a moved wall, a second story) and your assistant builds it as a design layer, so you can switch between it and the house as drawn. You don't drag walls in the browser; the assistant models the change and checks it.

Are the studies professional advice?

No. They are researched, cited write-ups to help you think and ask better questions of your architect, engineer or contractor.

What if my plans are scans?

That works. Scanned and CAD-drawn sheets are read from sharp close-ups, and each number is tied to the image it came from so you can check it.
